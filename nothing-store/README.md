# Nothing Store

A slick, modern website that sells absolutely nothing - because sometimes, nothing is exactly what you need.

## Features

- **Premium Nothing**: 100% authentic emptiness, no additives
- **Instant Delivery**: Your nothing arrives immediately (or doesn't)
- **Flexible Payment Options**: Choose from 5 different payment frequencies
- **Modern Design**: Sleek gradients, animations, and responsive layout
- **Fully Responsive**: Works perfectly on all devices

## How to Use

1. Open `index.html` in your web browser
2. Browse the features of our premium nothing
3. Select your preferred payment plan
4. Click "Buy Nothing Now" to complete your purchase of nothing
